Methodology, Telemetry Sources & Transparency Notice

Observed prices are ingested directly from Agmarknet (Directorate of Marketing & Inspection, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Govt of India) and verified against authorized APMC electronic auction ledgers (e-NAM gateways). Modal prices indicate the most frequent transaction value occurring during the primary trading session. Projected trends represent AI statistical econometric forecasting based on historical seasonality, supply arrival gradients, and IMD rainfall indices.

Disclaimer: AgriSync market intelligence is engineered for operational harvest and dispatch decision support. Not construed as statutory commodity trading or financial advisory.
