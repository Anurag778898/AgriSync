## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-precision agricultural telemetry and AI intelligence. It balances rural utility with enterprise computational rigor—grounded, reliable, and sharp.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative & Scientific:** Grounded in agronomical science, predictive modeling, and absolute clarity.
- **Organic Modernism:** Merges dense enterprise SaaS workflows with rich, grounded earth tones instead of generic cold tech blues.
- **Operational Clarity:** High signal-to-noise ratio designed for high-stress operational windows (planting, harvest, risk events) across desktop control rooms and in-field tablets.

### Design Movement
- **Precision Data Minimalism:** Highly organized grid structures, explicit hierarchy, minimal decorative noise, and purposeful color coding for telemetry and risk matrices.

## Colors

The palette grounds modern dashboard design in natural, land-informed tones, balancing warm organic backdrops with sharp alert signals.

### Color Tokens & Semantic Roles
- **Primary (`#174B35` - Forest Green):** Anchors brand presence, main navigation rail, primary buttons, and key interactive titles.
- **Secondary (`#3F7D46` - Leaf Green):** Positive telemetry status, crop health markers, and sub-navigation indicators.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#C5E86C` - Vibrant Lime):** AI recommendation triggers, highlight badges, selected states, and precision visual markers.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#F7F6EF` - Warm Ivory):** Global page background; relieves optical fatigue caused by pure white canvases while preserving natural contrast.
- **Neutral Card Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** High-density component cards, floating panels, dropdowns, and data grids.
- **Primary Text (`#26352F` - Deep Slate):** High contrast legibility for primary values, metric headers, and body copy.
- **Muted Secondary Text (`#7A8E83` - Slate Moss):** Descriptive labels, column headers, units of measurement, and disabled states.
- **Structural Border (`#D7E1D8` - Pale Moss Border):** Subtle dividers, card contours, table gridlines, and inactive field boundaries.

### Functional / Telemetry Accents
- **Weather / Moisture (`#2B6CB0` - Hydro Blue):** Rainfall levels, soil saturation, and weather forecasts.
- **Warning / Advisory (`#D97706` - Amber Warning):** Drought stress alerts, machinery servicing notices, and pest risk flags.
- **Critical Alert (`#DC2626` - Alert Red):** Immediate frost hazards, irrigation failures, and systemic warnings.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy uses a tripartite structure suited for complex analytical tools:

- **Manrope:** Provides structural warmth, precision curves, and executive polish for high-level summaries and section headers.
- **Inter:** The interface engine. Calibrated for microcopy, data tables, multi-column layouts, and form fields where maximum legibility at small sizes is critical.
- **Monospace Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono` / `IBM Plex Mono` equivalents):** Applied across sensor readouts, GPS coordinates, moisture percentages, acreage calculations, and tabular row cells to ensure numeric alignment across varying decimal places.

## Layout & Spacing

A strict 8px spatial grid underpins high layout density without visual overcrowding.

### Grid & Layout Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) outer margin. Left navigation rail is docked at 260px (or collapsed to 72px for expanded map views).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) outer margin. Side-by-side data cards wrap to two columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) margins. Dense tables shift to horizontal scroll containers or stacked metric cards.

### Density Strategy
- Spacing within dense telemetry cards uses `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) gaps to preserve visual affinity between values and their units.
- Macro layout distances rely on `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) to distinguish functional sectors (e.g., Yield Prediction vs. Soil Moisture).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through quiet, low-contrast structural outlines paired with subtle, organic ambient shadows.

### Atmospheric Layering
- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Warm Ivory (`#F7F6EF`). Flat, no shadow.
- **Surface Level 1 (Default Data Containers):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid perimeter of `#D7E1D8`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover / Drawer):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with border `#D7E1D8`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Dropdowns / Flyout Panels / Tooltips):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with border `#D7E1D8`. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.05)`.
- **Dark Inversion Level (Maps / Imagery Overlays):** Deep Forest Green (`#174B35`) with 85% opacity, backdrop blur of 8px, and 1px border of `rgba(197, 232, 108, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances precision tech with natural forms through moderate, refined corner radii.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** Standardized on `0.625rem` - `0.75rem` (10px - 12px) radii, softening dense analytical panels without wasting interactive area.
- **Data Display Containers (Charts, Satellite Views):** `0.875rem` (14px) outer boundaries for card containers.
- **Pills & Status Tags:** Fully rounded ends (`9999px`) for micro-indicators, AI recommendations, and numeric telemetry chips to visually decouple status indicators from structural rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest Green (`#174B35`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, 12px radius, medium weight. Hover shifts to `#0F3324`. Active state scales subtly (`transform: scale(0.99)`).
- **Secondary / Action Outlined:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#D7E1D8`, Slate (`#26352F`) text. Hover introduces `#F7F6EF` background and border color shifts to `#7A8E83`.
- **Accent AI Action:** Accent Lime (`#C5E86C`) background, Slate (`#26352F`) text, semi-bold. Used strictly for automated recommendations and trigger actions.

### Cards & Panels
- **Structure:** Surface White (`#FFFFFF`), 12px to 14px border radius, 1px border (`#D7E1D8`). 
- **Content Padding:** 16px internal padding for metric widgets; 24px internal padding for macro reporting blocks.
- **Header Divider:** Flat horizontal divider using `#D7E1D8` separating titles from data tables.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Numeric Inputs:** Pure White background, 1px solid border (`#D7E1D8`), Slate text, 10px radius. On focus: 1.5px border in Forest Green (`#174B35`) with an ambient outer glow ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unselected border `#7A8E83`. Selected state uses Forest Green (`#174B35`) fill with pure white tick marks. Checkbox corners rounded to 4px.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Height 24px, 9999px radius, padding 2px 10px. 
  - *Optimal:* Background `#3F7D46` at 12% opacity, text `#3F7D46`.
  - *Advisory:* Background `#D97706` at 12% opacity, text `#D97706`.
  - *Hazard:* Background `#DC2626` at 12% opacity, text `#DC2626`.
  - *AI Insight:* Background `#C5E86C`, text `#174B35`, bold tracking.

### Data Grids & Telemetry Tables
- **Header:** Background `#F7F6EF`, text `#7A8E83`, uppercase 11px Inter, tracking 0.04em, height 36px.
- **Row:** Height 44px (condensed) or 56px (standard), background `#FFFFFF`, bottom border 1px solid `#D7E1D8`.
- **Numeric Values:** Displayed strictly in Monospace with right-alignment.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Field Health Gauge (NDVI Index):** Segmented bar gauge using color ramp (`#DC2626` -> `#D97706` -> `#C5E86C` -> `#3F7D46` -> `#174B35`) with tabular readout and floating cursor pill.
- **AI Prescription Insight Card:** Highlighted with a left border accent (3px solid `#C5E86C`), containing confidence percentage tags and single-click parameter application controls.
